Soundline Arc

Quiet, by design.

Wireless over-ear headphones with adaptive noise cancelling and a 60-hour battery.

Scroll to explore

Design

Machined, not moulded.

The yokes and caps start as solid blocks of aluminium and are cut to shape, then bead-blasted and anodised. The weight sits low and close to your head, so 268 grams feels like less.

Inside

Seven layers, one sound.

Take an Arc apart and every layer has a job, from the foam against your ear to the light on the cap.

Sound

Big sound, small cone.

A 40 mm driver with a beryllium-coated dome: stiff enough not to flex, light enough to stop the moment a note does. Bass you feel, highs that don't hiss.

Made in your browser with the Web Audio API. Watch the driver move.

Noise cancelling

Turn the world down.

Six microphones hear the room around you, and the Arc chip plays back its exact opposite. Keep scrolling to switch it on.
